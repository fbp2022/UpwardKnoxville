The Forge

The Forge is Upward’s men’s discipleship group, focused on biblical leadership, discipline, accountability, brotherhood, and spiritual refinement. It exists for men who want to stop drifting, live intentionally before God, and step into the responsibility He calls them to carry.

Formation here is personal before it is public. We care about integrity in the quiet places, stewardship of time and relationships, and a steady readiness to serve where God has placed you. The tone is serious about sin and grace, grounded in Scripture, and committed to walking in humility with a few faithful brothers.

This is not a performance. It is a place to be known, corrected kindly when you need it, and encouraged to keep your eyes on Christ. If you are looking for reactionary noise or a stage for ego, you will not find it here. If you are looking for honest accountability and mature conversation around God’s Word, you might be in the right room.

What we emphasize

Biblical leadership that serves rather than postures. Responsibility at home, at work, and in the church. Discipline of mind and habit that supports prayer and the Word. Brotherhood that makes it harder to hide and easier to repent.

What you can expect

Calm, clear, convicting conversation. Space to admit struggle without celebrating sin. Respect for your time and intelligence. An invitation to grow in spiritual maturity at a sustainable pace, alongside others who are doing the same.
