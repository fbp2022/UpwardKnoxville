Men’s discipleship

The Forge

Live

The Forge is live. This is Upward’s men’s discipleship group, focused on biblical leadership, discipline, accountability, brotherhood, and spiritual refinement, for men who want to stop drifting, live intentionally before God, and step into the responsibility He calls them to carry. You can connect with brothers and the weekly rhythm through our Discord server below.

Formation here is personal before it is public. We care about integrity in the quiet places, stewardship of time and relationships, and a steady readiness to serve where God has placed you. The tone is serious about sin and grace, grounded in Scripture, and committed to walking in humility with a few faithful brothers.

This is not a performance. It is a place to be known, corrected kindly when needed, encouraged faithfully, and sharpened by the Word of God.

Staying connected

Most group interaction and participation currently takes place through Discord. This helps reduce unnecessary travel, allows people with different schedules to participate more consistently, and makes it easier for individuals both inside and outside the Knoxville area to stay connected and involved.

While there may occasionally be planned in-person gatherings, fellowship events, or meetups connected to these groups, those would typically be scheduled well in advance with clear communication beforehand.

Our goal is not to replace real fellowship, but to create practical and accessible ways for believers to stay connected, encouraged, sharpened by Scripture, and involved in discipleship throughout the week.
