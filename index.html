Announcements

Who We Are

We are ordinary believers who want to keep Christ at the center. No pressure, no performance, and no pretending. Just a welcoming place to read Scripture faithfully and encourage one another in real life.

A Welcoming Atmosphere

You are welcome to listen quietly, ask questions, and learn at your own pace. We value authenticity, humility, and a fellowship rhythm that helps people grow over time.

Why Upward Exists

Upward Knoxville is a Bible-centered fellowship in Knoxville where ordinary believers gather to study the Scriptures together.

We teach the Bible verse by verse, chapter by chapter, so that God’s Word can speak clearly into our lives. Our desire is to grow in simple faithfulness, praying together, asking honest questions, and encouraging one another in Christ.

Everyone is welcome. Whether you are new to the Bible or have walked with Jesus for years, there is a place for you here to listen, learn, and grow.

How We Teach

We teach line by line and verse by verse with context in view. Scripture interprets Scripture. God’s written Word comes first, not motivational speaking or performance-driven Christianity.

Our Current Stage

Upward Knoxville is currently a growing Bible teaching and fellowship community in Knoxville, Tennessee. It is not yet operating as a full church body, but the hope is to grow faithfully and prayerfully as the Lord leads.

What to Expect

Bring your Bible and something to take notes with, if desired.
Bibles are not provided yet because Upward is new and starting simple.
Children and families are welcome; parents remain responsible for their children.
No childcare is currently provided.
Mature topics may occasionally arise, with advance communication when appropriate.
No one is pressured to speak. Questions are welcome and teachings are posted online.
