About Upward Knoxville

Upward Knoxville exists to help people grow closer to Jesus Christ through Scripture, fellowship, and discipleship in a calm and authentic environment.

Mission

To open the Bible faithfully, verse by verse, and encourage believers to walk with Christ in everyday life.

Vision

A grounded fellowship where ordinary people are transformed by God's Word and strengthened in Christian community.

Values

Scripture first, authenticity, humility, prayerful growth, and fellowship marked by grace and truth.

Purpose

To create a welcoming place in Knoxville where people can learn Scripture clearly, ask honest questions, and mature in Christ.
