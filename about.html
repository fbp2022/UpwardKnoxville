The vision for Upward Knoxville

Why Upward Exists

I long for something steady in a noisy world: ordinary believers opening the Bible together and letting God speak for Himself. Upward Knoxville began with a simple burden—to teach Scripture line by line, verse by verse, keep context in view, and let the Word set the pace instead of trends or personalities.

I am wary of performance Christianity and talks that sparkle but drift from what the Lord has actually written. What I want alongside brothers and sisters is quiet faithfulness: prayer, honest questions when they come, and fellowship that does not pressure anyone to pretend they have arrived.

This is not a stage, a brand launch, or an attempt to impress Knoxville. It is a small Scripture-centered fellowship where you can listen, learn, and grow at a humane rhythm. If you love Jesus and want His Word opened patiently among His people, that is the spirit of what we are praying toward here—nothing more, nothing less.

Mission

To open the Bible faithfully, verse by verse, and encourage believers to walk with Christ in everyday life.

Scripture first

Teaching and conversation stay tethered to the text—context, clarity, and humility before the Lord.

Fellowship

Real encouragement, prayer, and patience with one another as we grow—not hype, not pressure.

Simplicity

A calm rhythm: ordinary people, an open Bible, and room for the Spirit to work over time in Knoxville.
