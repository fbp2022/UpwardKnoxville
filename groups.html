Discipleship

Groups

Upward Knoxville is beginning with Bible teaching and fellowship. As the fellowship grows, groups provide smaller, focused spaces for discipleship, encouragement, accountability, prayer, and spiritual growth.

Staying connected

Most group interaction and participation currently takes place through Discord. This helps reduce unnecessary travel, allows people with different schedules to participate more consistently, and makes it easier for individuals both inside and outside the Knoxville area to stay connected and involved.

While there may occasionally be planned in-person gatherings, fellowship events, or meetups connected to these groups, those would typically be scheduled well in advance with clear communication beforehand.

Our goal is not to replace real fellowship, but to create practical and accessible ways for believers to stay connected, encouraged, sharpened by Scripture, and involved in discipleship throughout the week.

Groups Are Developing

Upward Knoxville is still in its early stages overall, and we are adding discipleship spaces slowly and intentionally. The Forge is already live for men; other spaces (such as The Foundry) are still forming as the Lord provides leadership and need. We do not want to create programs simply to appear established.

These groups are not meant to replace the main Bible teaching and fellowship of Upward Knoxville. They are meant to support it by creating smaller spaces where believers can grow honestly, encourage one another, ask questions, pray, and walk more faithfully with Christ.
