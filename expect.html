What to Expect

A relaxed and welcoming fellowship centered on Scripture.

Bring your Bible, and bring something to take notes with if desired.
Bibles are not provided yet because Upward is new and starting simple.
Verse-by-verse teaching is central, and context is emphasized.
Children and families are welcome.
Childcare is not currently provided, and parents remain responsible for their children.
Mature topics may occasionally be taught; advance communication will be shared when appropriate.
No one is pressured to speak. Questions are welcome.
Teachings will be posted online afterward.
