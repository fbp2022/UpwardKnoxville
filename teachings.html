How we teach

We teach the Bible verse by verse, chapter by chapter, with context kept in view. Our aim is patient exposition—careful observation, clarity, and application that follows what Scripture says—so Christ remains central as we open the Word together.

We teach line by line and verse by verse with context in view. Scripture interprets Scripture. God’s written Word comes first, not motivational speaking or performance-driven Christianity.
